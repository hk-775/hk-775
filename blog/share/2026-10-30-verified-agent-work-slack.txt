New write-up: Improving Agents Starts with Verifying the Work

I used RSI Engine and AutoHelix to compare completion counters with verified outputs in a synthetic AI workflow. Verified feedback achieved 120/120 correct outcomes in every run, including justified handoffs.

For SAs: define “done” through observable effects, keep grading outside the agent’s control, and distinguish completed work from correct escalation.
