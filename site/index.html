What this does—and does not—measure

These two runs check a specific data boundary. The completion flag does not assess summary quality. C4 outputs a redaction placeholder. There is no live-model quality evaluation, real human approval, production traffic, isolation certification, or demonstrated business saving in this example.

Separate evidence: AxonLLM routing classification

A September 16, 2026 evaluation on 60 held-out generated prompts reported 52 correct heuristic routes, 55 LLM routes, and 57 hybrid routes. The hybrid made 17 classifier calls. That experiment used live classifier calls on synthetic prompts and excluded downstream answer generation.
