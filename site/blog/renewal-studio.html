By Harleen Kaur · All 9 parts published · 28 interactive workflows

A customer accepts. Billing updates. Access fails. What should happen next? Start with a standard renewal, then follow the decisions that connect retention, finance, agents and operations.

Explore the diagrams. Then try the application.

28 interactive workflows include scenario choices, moving connectors, solid traveling dots and SVG, PNG and editable draw.io downloads. The browser figures are local teaching examples; the linked application runs with synthetic data and simulated providers.
